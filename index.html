🎮 Your roster

💰 Finances

Wins pay $2,000 in the league. Weekly operating costs, staff salaries, academy upkeep and $750 travel costs per series apply. Major and league prizes pay out at the end. Falling more than $10,000 into debt means bankruptcy.

🔁 Transfer market

Sign players onto your bench (max 3), then press Start to swap them with a starter of the same role. Lowball: 80% salary, 40% chance they accept, otherwise they leave. Stars show growth potential. Young players improve each year, players 30+ decline.

🏆 Season schedule

🧠 Staff market

Hire specialists on two-year contracts. Staff improve training, match preparation, scouting or morale, but their salaries are paid every week. You can employ up to four staff members.

🏢 Your staff

Staff contracts last two years. Renew before expiry to keep their bonuses.

🔎 Scouting network

Scouting reports cost money and reveal more reliable estimates. Your Scout improves report quality. Prospects can be recruited into your youth academy.

🎓 Youth academy

Academy players develop slowly each year. Promote a prospect to the bench when ready; the normal roster and role rules still apply. Academy upkeep is included in weekly running costs.

📈 Brand & audience

Your audience grows from competitive success and content. Followers improve sponsor offers and merchandise sales. Brand income is included in weekly finances.

🤝 Sponsor market

Sign up to three sponsors. Deals pay every match-week and last eight series; higher-profile organisations attract better offers.

🛡️ Tier 2 team

Your development squad competes below the main roster. Its players improve through matches and can be promoted to your senior bench or sold to another organisation.

Tier 2 recruitment

🌐 Competitive ecosystem

Your Tier 2 squad competes in a separate eight-team development league. Results update after each senior series, and the table resets at the start of each season.

🤝 Transfer desk

Rival clubs can make buyout offers for bench players. Accept to sell, or negotiate a higher fee. Senior starters are protected from accidental roster gaps.

🧠 Team identity & tactics

Choose your organisation's preferred approach. It gives a small, persistent match modifier; you can still change tactics during matches.

🏗️ Facilities

🎮 Multi-game division

Expand into additional esports titles. Each division requires an upfront setup fee and adds weekly operating costs, but builds reputation and can generate income through simulated competitions.
